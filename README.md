# Codebook:
This codebook consists of data regarding meteorological winter events, with a focus on severe winter storm
conditions and cold waves. The area from which the data comes is Europe, which is then separated into
four Subregions, Western Europe, Eastern Europe, Southern Europe, and Northern Europe. The data is
from the years between 2000 and 2021 for each country where there was a recorded qualifying event. To
determine the impact of winter weather per country, some variables are examined, including: the number of
people affected and deaths, economic damage, and average temperatures. The way this data is set up allows
for easy combination with other datasets (such as population to determine economic losses per capita, or
governance scores to determine whether stronger governance affects economic losses or total deaths, etc).
Data is in descending order, starting from the country with the most meteorological winter events.

Data Source: EM-DAT, CRED / UCLouvain, 2025, Brussels, Belgium.
